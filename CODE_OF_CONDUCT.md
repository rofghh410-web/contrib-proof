# Code of Conduct

ContribProof is a technical project for people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, describe problems precisely, and assume good faith while still checking evidence carefully.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project constructive.

To report a concern, contact the maintainers through the private channel listed in `SECURITY.md` or the repository owner profile. Reports are handled as privately as practical.
